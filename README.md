Coke Sales Analysis (2021)

An Excel based exploratory analysis of beverage sales and operating margin across key American Coca-Cola retailers during 2021. The workbook includes the source data and summary tables for exploring trends by month, brand, region, and retailer.

## Workbook

Download and open [Coke-sales-analysis-solution.xlsx](Coke-sales-analysis-solution.xlsx) in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

The workbook contains these tabs:

- **Data** — transaction-level sales records and calculated fields.
- **Beginner** — summary tables for monthly sales, region, retailer, brand, and units sold.
- **Intermediate** — sales and unit summaries by brand, month, and region.
- **Advanced** — monthly unit totals by region and retailer/brand unit summaries.
- **Addtional Analysis** — suggested follow-up questions for deeper analysis.

## Dataset overview

- **Period:** January–December 2021
- **Records:** 3,888
- **Retailers:** 4
- **Beverage brands:** 6 — Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Sprite, Fanta, Powerade, and Dasani Water
- **Geography:** 5 regions, 50 states, and 52 cities

The source data includes retailer and retailer ID, invoice date, region, state, city, beverage brand, price per unit, units sold, total sales, operating profit, operating margin, and month.

## Analysis included

The summary tables support exploration of monthly sales trends, sales by region and retailer, brand revenue and volume, brand sales by month and region, and monthly units sold by region.

The **Addtional Analysis** tab lists possible next steps, such as margin stability and revenue versus profit comparisons. These are suggestions for future work and are not presented as completed analyses in the workbook.

## Key findings

- Total sales for 2021 are **8,684,027.50** (as recorded in the workbook).
- **December** had the highest monthly sales at **1,045,775.00**; **February** had the lowest at **484,975.00**.
- The **West** region led sales with **2,835,100.00**.
- **Coca-Cola** had the highest brand sales at **2,015,890.00** and the highest units sold at **4,133,250**.

## Calculation notes

The workbook calculates **Total Sales** as `Price per Unit × Units Sold` and **Operating Profit** as `Total Sales × Operating Margin`. Figures above are reported in the workbook's units; no currency symbol is specified in the source sheet.
